About

A home built for a Kenyan holiday

Room to spread out, comforts that just work, and the mountain framed from the garden.

The house

Nanyuki Country Home is a spacious, modern property in one of Kenya’s most rewarding travel regions. The layout is generous and light: an open living area that flows to the garden, seven comfortable bedrooms that sleep up to 16, and quiet corners to read or work.

It was made for the rhythm of travel here: pre-dawn game drives, unhurried breakfasts, and evenings under a wide Laikipia sky. You take the whole place for your stay, with no shared spaces and no crowds.

The setting

Nanyuki sits on the equator at the base of Mt Kenya, the gateway to conservancies, forest trails and some of the country’s best wildlife. The town has everything you need close at hand, while the house stays private and calm.
